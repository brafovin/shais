# 3D model attribution

`m4-competition.glb` is an optimised version (WebP textures, meshopt
compression) of:

- **Title:** 2021 BMW M4 Competition
- **Author:** Ricy ([@ngon_3d](https://sketchfab.com/ngon_3d))
- **Source:** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2021-bmw-m4-competition-d3f07b471d9f4a2c9a2acf79d88a3645
- **License:** [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) —
  credit the author, **non-commercial use only**.

Changes: textures resized/re-encoded, geometry compressed. At runtime the site
recolours the paint, wheels and brake calipers and adds a carbon rear wing,
front splitter and canards.
